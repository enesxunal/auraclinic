Cookie Policy

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help the site function or remember preferences.

Cookies we use

Name / storagePurposeDuration
aura_clinic_cookie_consent_v1 (localStorage) Remembers that you accepted the cookie notice Until you clear browser data
Session / technical cookies (hosting) Basic operation and security of the website Session or short-term
_fbp, _fbc (Meta Pixel cookies, after consent) Measures visits and ad performance for Aura Clinic campaigns Up to 90 days (Meta)
Google Ads / gtag cookies (after consent) Measures Google ad clicks, conversions and site usage Varies (Google)

Analytics & marketing

If you accept analytics cookies, we may load the Meta Pixel and Google Ads tags (and GA4 if configured) to measure visits, form submissions, WhatsApp clicks, and AI preview usage. You can Accept, Reject, or Manage preferences on the banner.

You can change your choice later via the cookie policy page or by clearing site data. Declining analytics does not block essential site features.

Managing cookies

You can delete cookies and localStorage in your browser settings. Blocking all cookies may limit site functionality.
